A protein-rich breakfast keeps you full longer and makes weekday mornings simpler. The trick is to prepare it once on the weekend, so the decision is already made when you wake up.

## 1. Overnight oats with Greek yogurt

Mix rolled oats, Greek yogurt, milk of your choice and a spoon of chia seeds in a jar. Leave it in the fridge overnight, then add berries in the morning. Greek yogurt adds a good amount of protein without much extra prep.

## 2. Egg and vegetable muffins

Whisk eggs with spinach, peppers and a little cheese, then bake in a muffin tin for about 20 minutes. They keep for four days in the fridge and reheat in under a minute.

## 3. Cottage cheese bowl

Spoon cottage cheese into a bowl, top with sliced fruit and a handful of nuts. It needs no cooking, and you can prepare the fruit on Sunday so the bowl comes together in a minute.

## 4. Chicken and sweet potato hash

Dice sweet potato and cook it with cubed chicken breast, onion and a pinch of paprika. Portion it into containers and warm it in the morning. Add a fried egg on busy days.

## 5. Tofu scramble

Crumble firm tofu into a hot pan with turmeric, onion and any vegetables you have. It's a useful option for plant-based eaters, and it stores well for three days.

## Tips for a smooth week

- Cook on one day and portion everything into labelled containers.
- Choose two breakfasts you like and rotate them, so you don't get bored.
- Keep a simple protein source in the fridge for mornings you run late.

Quantities depend on your daily targets. If you're managing a health condition, check the plan with your healthcare professional.
